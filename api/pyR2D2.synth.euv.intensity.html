intensity

pyR2D2.synth.euv.intensity(em, response='aia', channels=None, logte_edges=array([5., 5.05, 5.1, 5.15, 5.2, 5.25, 5.3, 5.35, 5.4, 5.45, 5.5, 5.55, 5.6, 5.65, 5.7, 5.75, 5.8, 5.85, 5.9, 5.95, 6., 6.05, 6.1, 6.15, 6.2, 6.25, 6.3, 6.35, 6.4, 6.45, 6.5, 6.55, 6.6, 6.65, 6.7, 6.75, 6.8, 6.85, 6.9, 6.95, 7., 7.05, 7.1, 7.15, 7.2, 7.25, 7.3, 7.35, 7.4, 7.45, 7.5, 7.55, 7.6, 7.65, 7.7, 7.75, 7.8, 7.85, 7.9, 7.95, 8.]), nsub=20)[source]

箱ごとの EM から強度を作る。

Parameters:

  • em (array (..., nbin)) – log10 T の箱ごとの EM [cm^-5] (最後の軸が箱)。d.vc.em_x など

  • response (str, Path or ResponseTable) – 応答の表 (load_response())。既定は同梱の AIA

  • channels (list of str, optional) – 作るチャンネル。既定は表の全部

  • logte_edges (array (nbin + 1,)) – 箱の境界。既定は R2D2plus の vl_dem.dac のもの

Returns:

チャンネル名 → 強度 (単位は応答 × cm^-5。既定の表では DN s^-1 pix^-1)

Return type:

dict of str to array (…)
